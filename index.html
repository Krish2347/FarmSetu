ABOUT FARMSETU

Connecting Farms. Creating Fairer Markets.

FarmSetu is a digital marketplace designed to bring farmers and buyers closer by reducing unnecessary intermediaries in the agricultural supply chain.

We believe that farmers deserve better returns for their hard work, while consumers deserve access to quality agricultural products at fair prices. FarmSetu creates a transparent space where farmers can list their produce directly and buyers can discover, compare and request products with greater confidence.
